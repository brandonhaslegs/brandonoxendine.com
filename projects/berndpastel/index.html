Bernd Pastel

A website for my wife’s nail business. The design is meant to reflect old Myspace-culture and “junk nails” while communicating that she likes to make colorful, fun nail sets.

Role: Visual Design, Branding, Code

View it live: berndpastel.com

The site switches between German and English based on the user’s device language settings.

Pricing is organized by service level to set expectations up front, with real nail sets shown via lightweight carousels.

The location page grounds the brand in Berlin-Neukolln, with some fun shots of our apartment where she does nails.

Contact CTAs are prominent and repeated throughout the site to make booking frictionless.

And the site is of course fully responsive with a special mobile navigation.
